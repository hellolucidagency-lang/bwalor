About Lucid

Systems and stories, built with the same discipline.

Two crafts, one studio — AI automation and visual production, run by people who ship.

Who we are

Lucid Agency was founded on a simple observation: most businesses either automate without a brand worth automating for, or they build a beautiful brand that still runs on manual busywork behind the scenes. We exist to close that gap — engineering the systems that run your operations, and producing the visuals that make people trust what they see.

What we believe

Automation should feel invisible, not gimmicky. A workflow is only good if the person on the other end never has to think about it. And a photo or video is only good if it earns attention it can back up. We hold both crafts to that same standard: quiet, dependable engineering, and visuals with intent behind every frame.

How we're built

Our team is small and senior by design — automation engineers, prompt and systems architects, photographers, editors, and strategists who've done this inside agencies and in-house teams before joining Lucid. Every engagement is led by someone who will still be answering your messages six months in.

Leadership

Lucid Agency is directed by Laila Nasser, who oversees strategy and client relationships across every engagement. You can reach her directly via WhatsApp using the contact details in our footer.
