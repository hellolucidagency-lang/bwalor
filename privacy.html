Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Lucid Agency ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms, email, or WhatsApp — such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Project details you share with us during a strategy call or onboarding.
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, browser type, general location) collected automatically when you browse our site.

How We Use It

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule strategy calls.
  • To deliver and improve the automation and production services you engage us for.
  • To send occasional updates about our work, only if you've opted in.

How We Protect It

We limit access to personal data to team members who need it to do their jobs, and we do not sell your information to third parties. Data shared with tools we use to run our business (such as scheduling or CRM software) is governed by those providers' own security practices.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@hellolucidagency.com.

Cookies

Our site may use minimal cookies or local storage to remember basic preferences and understand aggregate traffic. These do not identify you personally.

Contact
